# Ronyxx: ask your Odoo, get exact figures

Ronyxx answers questions about your company from its Odoo ERP: sales and revenue, what customers owe and what's overdue, what you owe suppliers, cash and bank, profit and loss, VAT, orders, purchases, stock and deliveries. Every figure is worked out by Ronyxx's own tested code from your Odoo records, never estimated by the AI, and Ronyxx only reads: it never changes anything in Odoo.

Ask in your own words, or run a ready-made review:

- "Do my month-end review"
- "Who should I chase for payment today?"
- "What should I reorder?"
- "Give me today's numbers"

Each person sees only what their role allows. Works with Odoo 16 to 20.

## The reviews

- **Month-end review**: last month's sales against the month before, profit and loss, margin, what customers owe and how much is overdue, what you owe suppliers, VAT, and goods received but not billed yet.
- **Who to chase**: overdue invoices by customer, the oldest first, and short reminder emails for you to send yourself.
- **Stock to reorder**: products running low, days of stock left, what's already on order and late receipts.
- **Daily numbers**: yesterday's sales, cash in the bank, overdue invoices, low stock and late deliveries.

Each review asks Ronyxx a few questions one at a time and shows every answer exactly as Ronyxx gave it. Claude never changes, rounds or adds up Ronyxx's figures.

## What you need

A Ronyxx account with your company's Odoo connected, and your work email on your company's Ronyxx team. Start at https://ronyxx.com. Until your Odoo is connected, Ronyxx answers from its sample company and says so in every answer.

## What it does with your data

- The plugin connects Claude to Ronyxx's server at https://mcp.ronyxx.com/mcp, after you sign in to Ronyxx. Your questions go there and nowhere else.
- Ronyxx only reads your Odoo. It never creates, changes or deletes anything, and it answers only what your role in your company may see.
- The reviews are instructions to Claude. They run no code on your computer.
