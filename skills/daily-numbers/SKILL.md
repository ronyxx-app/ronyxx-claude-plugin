# Daily numbers

Ask Ronyxx these questions with the `ask` tool, one at a time, each complete on its own:

1. What were our sales yesterday?
2. What is our bank and cash balance today?
3. How much is overdue from customers?
4. Which 3 customers owe us the most overdue?
5. Which products are running low?
6. Which deliveries to customers are late?

Then show the person the answers under short headings (Sales, Cash, Overdue, Stock, Deliveries), each exactly as Ronyxx gave it.

Rules:
- Never change, round, add up or combine Ronyxx's figures, and never work out a figure of your own. If two answers seem to need joining, ask Ronyxx one more question instead.
- If Ronyxx can't answer one of them (an app not installed, or the person's role can't see it), say so in one line under that heading and go on.
- If Ronyxx says it is answering from its sample company (the person's Odoo isn't connected yet), say that once at the top.
- End with at most two follow-up questions the person could ask, each one plain question about their records.
