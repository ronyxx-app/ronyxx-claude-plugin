# Who to chase

Ask Ronyxx with the `ask` tool, one question at a time:

1. Which customers have overdue invoices, and how much is overdue for each? (the customers with the most overdue first)
2. Which invoices are more than 60 days overdue?

Show both answers exactly as Ronyxx gave them.

If the person wants reminders, write one short, polite email per customer they choose. Use only the invoice numbers, amounts and dates exactly as Ronyxx gave them for that customer. If a figure for that customer isn't in Ronyxx's answers, ask Ronyxx for it ("Which invoices does <customer> have overdue?") before writing.

Rules:
- Never change, round or add up Ronyxx's figures. A customer's total comes from Ronyxx, never from adding their invoices yourself.
- Ronyxx only reads Odoo: it doesn't send emails or change anything. Give the drafts to the person to send themselves.
- Keep each email under 120 words, in the person's language, with no threats or legal wording unless they ask for it.
