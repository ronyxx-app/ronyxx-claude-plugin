# Month-end review

Use last month unless the person names another month. Ask Ronyxx these questions with the `ask` tool, one at a time, each complete on its own and naming the month:

1. What were our sales last month compared with the month before?
2. What was our profit and loss last month?
3. What was our gross margin last month?
4. What do customers owe us now?
5. How much of what customers owe us is overdue?
6. What do we owe suppliers now?
7. How much of what we owe suppliers is overdue?
8. What was our net VAT for last month?
9. What have we received from suppliers but not been billed for yet?

Show each answer under its own heading, exactly as Ronyxx gave it, in this order.

Rules:
- Never change, round, add up or combine Ronyxx's figures, and never calculate a figure of your own (no totals across answers, no percentages Ronyxx didn't give). If the person wants such a figure, ask Ronyxx for it as one question.
- If an answer is about another period than the one asked, say so and ask Ronyxx again with the exact dates.
- If Ronyxx can't answer one of them, say so in one line under that heading and go on.
- After the answers, you may point to what stands out, in words, quoting Ronyxx's figures exactly as given.
